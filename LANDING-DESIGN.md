# Whatsend landing page: design and copy rationale

The codebase is a Next.js WhatsApp campaign app with CSV contact lists, message personalization and variants, multiple connected numbers, delivery analytics, and an inbox. The redesign focuses on that complete workflow.

## Research — October 11, 2026

- [Wati campaigns](https://www.wati.io/page/campaigns/): outcome-first headline, a tangible product visual, a clear primary action, and explanations tied to campaign tasks.
- [Respond.io WhatsApp](https://respond.io/integrations/whatsapp): messaging positioned around acquisition, conversion, retention, and managing conversations.
- [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/): a clear product proposition and product demonstrations that explain the experience.

These references inform design choices; their conversion performance has not been independently measured.

## Copy decisions

Lead with “Reach customers. Make sales on WhatsApp.” Explain what the app does immediately beneath it. Replace infrastructure jargon with user benefits. Use “Get started” consistently and send visitors to the existing authentication flow. Demonstrate personalization with three selectable examples. Label the preview as illustrative.

Remove unverified customer metrics, timing claims, free-trial claims, and the absolute ban guarantee. Do not invent customer logos, reviews, prices, or integrations. Answer practical objections in a native, keyboard-accessible FAQ.

## Visual direction

Warm off-white canvas, forest-green accents, dark-green workflow section, lime closing call to action. Strong editorial typography, generous whitespace, fine dividers, and a compact campaign preview. Responsive layout and reduced-motion support. Landing styles are scoped to avoid changing dashboard styles.

## Measurement after launch

Measure primary CTA clicks and completed signups. Compare the redesigned page with the prior page before claiming a conversion lift. Test headline alternatives only after establishing a baseline.

## Revision
Use the W mark from the existing OG image as the logo. Remove decorative labels, status dots, sparkles, the tilted preview, and slogan strips. Show a concrete sales offer and the buyer’s questions. Header actions are plain text links. Focus content on reaching customers, offers, delivery results, and buyer replies.
